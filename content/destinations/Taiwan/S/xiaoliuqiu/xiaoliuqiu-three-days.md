## The shape of the trip

The ferry from Donggang is short, but weather, tides, and water safety should control the plan. A new public island shuttle offers a lower-traffic alternative to putting every visitor on a scooter.

## Day 1 — Harbor and island orientation

- Check in near Baisha, learn the shuttle stops, and walk to Vase Rock outside the busiest hour.
- Watch sunset from an open coastal viewpoint without crossing barriers or standing on fragile reef.

## Day 2 — Water activity with a guide

- Use a licensed operator for snorkeling or diving and follow the assigned entry site.
- Keep distance from sea turtles; never touch, chase, feed, or block them.
- Join an intertidal visit only with a guide and only at the appropriate tide.

## Day 3 — Caves, coast, and ferry

- Choose Beauty Cave, Wild Boar Ditch, or Geban Bay rather than racing around every landmark.
- Leave a weather buffer before the return ferry and any onward train.

> Ferry service and water activity can be suspended. A cancelled session is the correct outcome when sea or weather conditions are unsafe.
