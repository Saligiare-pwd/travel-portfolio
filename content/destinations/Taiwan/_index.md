## A Taiwan shortlist for first-time visitors

Use Taipei as the gateway, then choose by interest rather than trying to cross the whole island too quickly.

- **Culture and food:** Tainan for historic lanes, temples, Anping, and a day built around small dishes.
- **Mountain day trip:** Qixingshan for a public-transport hike through Yangmingshan's volcanic landscape.
- **Forest escape:** Taipingshan for cloud forest, heritage trails, and an overnight away from the city.
- **Hot springs near Taipei:** Wulai for Atayal culture, the old street, waterfall, and a private hot-spring stop.
- **Coast and ocean:** Hualien for a relaxed east-coast base, or Xiaoliuqiu for snorkeling and diving with a licensed guide.
