## A one-day route

Wulai works best as one valley journey, not a list of scattered attractions. Use public transport, walk the old street first, and decide between the historic log cart and the riverside walk according to energy and weather.

## Morning — Old street and culture

- Take bus 849 from Xindian and start with the visitor centre or Atayal Museum.
- Share bamboo rice, millet mochi, wild-boar sausage, or dishes scented with mountain litsea; these are part of Wulai's Atayal food culture, not just snack stops.

## Afternoon — Waterfall and hot spring

- Walk or ride the 1.5-km log-cart line toward the waterfall district.
- Use the viewing area rather than entering closed riverbanks after rain.
- Reserve a legitimate private hot-spring room if bathing is important; do not treat the river as an unmanaged bath.

> Wulai roads and paths are sensitive to typhoons and heavy rain. Check closures and leave before the final bus.
