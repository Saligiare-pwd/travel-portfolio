## The recommended traverse

The national park lists the Xiaoyoukeng–Main Peak–East Peak–Lengshuikeng route as about 3.7 km, 380 m of elevation gain, and roughly 2.5 hours of walking. Add transport, breaks, and poor visibility rather than treating that figure as a deadline.

## How to pace it

- Start early from Jiantan and confirm which bus connection reaches Xiaoyoukeng that day.
- Expect exposed stone steps, sulphur vents, wind, and rapidly changing cloud near the ridge.
- Descend through East Peak to Lengshuikeng; do not reverse direction late merely to collect another photograph.

## Carry

Water, food, sun protection, a wind/rain layer, grippy footwear, and an offline map. In summer, avoid the afternoon-thunderstorm window; in autumn and winter, strong monsoon wind and fog are common official cautions.

> Turn back for thunder, severe wind, poor visibility, or any closure. This is a mountain route even though it sits inside Taipei.
