## Day 1 — Beethoven's Bonn

- Start around Münsterplatz, Bonn Minster, and the lanes of the centre.
- Visit Beethoven-Haus with enough time to listen rather than simply move through.
- Walk through the university's Hofgarten to the Rhine promenade.
- End in the Südstadt, where the streets become quieter and more residential.

## Day 2 — Museum Mile or Drachenfels

Choose according to weather:

- **Culture day:** Haus der Geschichte plus one art museum on the Museumsmeile.
- **Landscape day:** Train or tram toward Königswinter, then walk or take the railway toward Drachenfels.

## Stay, eat, move

- **Stay:** Near Bonn Hbf for a short visit; Südstadt for leafy streets and cafés.
- **Eat:** Bakery breakfast, a relaxed lunch near the market, and a Rhine-region wine tavern in the evening.
- **Move:** The centre is walkable; trams connect the station, Museum Mile, Bad Godesberg, and Königswinter.

> Bonn also makes a natural starting point for the Rhine route in the Jakobswege planner.
