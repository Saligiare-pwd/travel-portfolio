## Day 1 — Freiburg at street level

- Begin at Münsterplatz and the market, then look inside Freiburg Minster.
- Follow the Bächle through the old lanes rather than a fixed sightseeing circuit.
- Climb Schlossberg for the late-afternoon view if the weather is clear.
- Eat in the old town or continue into the quieter Wiehre district.

## Day 2 — Titisee by rail

- Take the regional train through the Höllental to Titisee station.
- Walk the quieter shore first; the promenade can wait until lunch.
- Choose a lake loop, a boat ride, or a short forest walk depending on weather.
- Return before the final rush so the train ride remains part of the day, not a race home.

## Stay, eat, move

- **Stay:** Freiburg Altstadt for atmosphere; near Hauptbahnhof for an early Black Forest departure.
- **Eat:** Market snacks in Freiburg, Black Forest cake only where you actually want to sit, and a lake lunch away from peak time.
- **Move:** Freiburg's centre is walkable. Titisee is connected by regional rail; check engineering works before travel.

> In German, the city is **Freiburg**, not Freiberg. Titisee belongs to the southern Black Forest east of Freiburg.
