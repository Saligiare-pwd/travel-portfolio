## Why this site exists

I work in astrophysics, and travel often begins with a conference, a research visit, or a train ticket between the two. This notebook keeps the part that happens outside the schedule: neighbourhood walks, mountain air, memorable meals, and routes I would gladly take again.

The plans are personal starting points rather than exhaustive guides. I prefer a clear shape for the day, room for weather and curiosity, and practical notes that make the journey easier to begin.
