The shape of the trip

Heidelberg is compact enough to explore without collecting a long checklist. Stay near the Altstadt or Bismarckplatz, walk one side of the Neckar each day, and leave space for the views.

Transport assumption

Heidelberg orientation

Assumes an off-peak start at Heidelberg Hbf. Walking times are approximate and the castle climb is slower with luggage.

Day 1 — Castle and Old Town

  • Morning: Begin at Marktplatz, then take the funicular or walk up to Heidelberg Castle.
  • Lunch: Return to the lanes around Hauptstraße for a simple café lunch.
  • Afternoon: Visit the university quarter, Student Prison, and Universitätsbibliothek.
  • Evening: Cross the Old Bridge at blue hour and have dinner back in the Altstadt.

Day 2 — Philosophenweg and the river

  • Morning: Cross the Theodor Heuss Bridge and climb the Philosophenweg slowly.
  • Midday: Descend into Neuenheim for lunch and a quieter neighbourhood atmosphere.
  • Afternoon: Follow the Neckar riverbank or visit the Kurpfälzisches Museum in poor weather.
  • Evening: Finish with the castle illuminated above the river.

Stay, eat, move

  • Stay: Altstadt for atmosphere; Bergheim around Bismarckplatz for easier tram and rail connections.
  • Eat: Look for a seasonal German menu, a bakery breakfast, and one unhurried riverside drink.
  • Move: The centre is best on foot. Trams are helpful between Heidelberg Hbf, Bismarckplatz, and the edge of the Old Town.

Check the official visitor page before departure for current castle, funicular, and museum hours.

Wing's travel notebook

Plans are deliberately unhurried: one neighbourhood, one long walk, one good meal at a time.
