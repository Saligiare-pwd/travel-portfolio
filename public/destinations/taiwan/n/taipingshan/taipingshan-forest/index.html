Taipingshan: two days in the cloud forest

Stay overnight to give Taipingshan Villa, a heritage trail, Cuifeng Lake, and Jiuzhize room to breathe.

Why stay overnight

The old notes included Jianqing Trail, Taipingshan Villa, Cuifeng Lake, and Jiuzhize Hot Spring. They are too spread out for a relaxed public-transport day trip. Two days work when lodging and the internal transfer are booked first.

Route

Taipingshan forest circuit

The park is large and internal access is not continuous. Confirm buses, lodging, train maintenance, and any Cuifeng transfer before arrival.

Stop 1 of 5

Luodong Station

Bus 1750 / car · 130 min to next stop

Day 1 — Arrival and forest history

  • Leave Luodong early and stop at Jiuzhize only if the day’s bus or driving plan allows it.
  • Check in near Taipingshan Villa, then choose the Bong Bong Train area or one short trail rather than racing to Cuifeng.

Day 2 — Jianqing or Cuifeng

  • Choose the atmospheric Jianqing Historic Trail for an easier morning, or pre-arrange access toward Cuifeng Lake.
  • Descend with a generous buffer; the mountain road is slow and weather can alter service.

The park’s official site posts live area and trail status. Check it before leaving Yilan and again before entering a trail.
