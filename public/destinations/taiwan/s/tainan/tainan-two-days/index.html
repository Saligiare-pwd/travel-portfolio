The shape of the trip

Tainan rewards short walks and frequent food stops. Share small portions—danzai noodles, beef soup, oyster fritters, spring rolls, fruit, or tofu pudding—so that eating supports the route instead of becoming a queueing contest.

Route

Tainan two-day orientation

Use walking for the central sights, then one bus or taxi transfer to Anping. Heat and meal queues are more important than the map’s ideal time.

Day 1 — The historic centre

  • Begin near the literature museum and Confucius Temple, then move through small streets toward Chihkan.
  • Alternate one historic site with one café or food stop. Do not schedule every famous shop.
  • Choose an evening market only if its operating day matches your visit.

Day 2 — Anping and the water

  • Give Anping Old Fort, the tree-house district, and old lanes a continuous block.
  • Stay toward the harbor for late light or a canal-area walk, then return to the centre once.

Many interiors and food shops have individual closing days. Check the official attraction page and the venue itself the evening before.
